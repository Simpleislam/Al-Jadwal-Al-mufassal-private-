# Privacy Policy: Al-Jadwal al-Mufassal

**App:** Al-Jadwal al-Mufassal (الجدول المفصّل في القراءات العشر)
**Package name:** `com.simpleislam.aljadwal`
**Developer:** simpleIslam
**Effective date:** 6 November 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Al-Jadwal al-Mufassal app for Android ("the app") handles information.
In short: **the app does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data.**

## Information we collect

None. The app has no account or sign-in, and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number,
location, contacts, photos, files or any other personal information.

The app contains:

- no analytics or tracking tools,
- no advertising and no advertising ID,
- no crash-reporting services,
- no third-party software development kits (SDKs) that collect data.

## How the app works

All the content of the app (the tables of al-Jadwal al-Mufassal, the index, the fonts and the PDF reader)
is packaged inside the app. The app works fully offline and does not send any information over the internet.

The app has Android's "internet" permission only because it displays its own built-in pages through Android's
web view component. It does not use this permission to connect to any server.

If you tap a link to an outside website, it opens in your phone's own web browser, outside the app.
That website's own privacy policy then applies.

## Information stored on your device

The app saves a small amount of information **only on your device** so that it works the way you left it:

- your chosen colour scheme, page colour and page view,
- the last page you opened, so you can continue where you stopped.

This information never leaves your device through the app. It is not linked to you and is not visible
to the developer. If you have turned on Android's own device backup, Android may include these settings in
your personal backup, under your Google account and Google's privacy policy.

You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage (Settings, Apps, Al-Jadwal, Storage, Clear storage)
or by uninstalling the app.

## Sharing of information

We do not share any information with third parties, because we do not collect any.

## Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Security

As no personal data is collected or transmitted, no personal data can be exposed by the app.
The settings saved on your device are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
